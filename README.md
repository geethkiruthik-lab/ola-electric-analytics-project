# Ola Electric – Service Crisis Analytics

## Business Analytics Capstone Project

### Project Overview
This project analyzes Ola Electric's service crisis, customer complaints, after-sales service performance, vehicle sales, and customer experience from January 2023 to December 2024.

The objective is to identify service bottlenecks, understand complaint trends, evaluate operational performance, and recommend data-driven improvements.

### Business Problem
Service delays, customer complaints, spare-parts availability, and service-centre capacity can affect customer satisfaction and brand performance. This project uses data analytics to investigate these challenges and identify areas for improvement.

### Tools & Technologies
- **SQL / PostgreSQL:** Data preparation, querying, KPI calculations, and analysis
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development and KPI visualization
- **Excel:** Data review and supporting analysis
- **Business Analysis:** Problem definition, insight generation, and recommendations

### Key Performance Indicators
- Market share
- Complaints per 1,000 vehicles
- Average turnaround time (TAT)
- Average customer satisfaction score (CSAT)
- Monthly sales and complaint trends
- Service-centre capacity and vehicle growth

### Key Findings
- The dashboard reports an Ola market-share KPI of 34.71%.
- The dashboard reports 51.63 complaints per 1,000 vehicles.
- Average turnaround time is 15.89, and average CSAT is 3.47.
- The analysis highlights complaint increases, service capacity, and the relationship between turnaround time and customer satisfaction.

*Note: KPI values reflect the current dashboard and should be interpreted according to the project's filters, definitions, and data sources.*

### Project Deliverables
- Power BI reports: `ola_electric_analysis.pbix` and `ola_sales.pbix`
- Dashboard preview: `ola_electric_dashboard.png.png`
- SQL analysis scripts
- Service crisis presentation: `Ola service crisis - New.pptx`

### Recommendations
1. Improve service-centre capacity in areas with high complaint volumes.
2. Monitor turnaround time and spare-parts availability.
3. Track complaints per 1,000 vehicles and customer satisfaction monthly.
4. Use dashboard KPIs to prioritize operational improvements.
5. Review sales and service metrics together to support business decisions.
